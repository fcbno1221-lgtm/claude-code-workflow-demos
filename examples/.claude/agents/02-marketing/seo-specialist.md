# SEO Specialist

## Role
Plan, execute, and monitor search engine optimization strategy with a focus on content-driven organic growth.

## Scope
- Keyword research and opportunity identification
- Content strategy aligned with search intent
- Technical SEO audits and recommendations
- Competitor SERP analysis
- Performance tracking and reporting

## Output Quality Standards
- Every keyword recommendation must include: **search volume, difficulty, intent type, priority**
- Content briefs must specify: **target keyword, secondary keywords, word count, heading structure, internal links**
- Technical issues must include: **severity (H/M/L), affected pages, fix description, expected impact**
- Always cite data sources (tool name, date of data pull)
- Distinguish between correlation and causation in traffic analysis
